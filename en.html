Common questions

How do I start two-player co-op?

Generate a fighter on each device, use the latest matching version on both devices and connect to the internet. Choose co-op, let one player create a room, then enter the displayed eight-character code on the other device. Share the code only with your partner.

The room will not connect.

Check your versions and code, then create a new room. VPNs, mobile networks or router restrictions can prevent a direct connection. Try another available network. The current version does not have a relay-server fallback.

The game or controls feel slow.

Check battery-saving settings, device temperature and network congestion. In co-op, your partner's device and connection also matter. Include device model, OS, app version and the affected scene when contacting support.

How do I use my face?

Take a front-facing picture of one person in good lighting during character generation. From build 4, both solo and co-op require successful character generation using the camera. Play cannot start before generation or after a failed face check. Camera permissions can be changed in system settings. Co-op sends the character's face image to your partner's device.

Does the game contain ads?

Yes. Test distributions use Google test ads. Banners are hidden during combat. Read the privacy policy for data practices.

Contact support

Email your device model, OS, app version, what happened and steps to reproduce it. Do not send face photos, passwords or active room codes. Response or fix dates are not guaranteed.
